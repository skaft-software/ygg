IBM Plex Mono copyright 2017 IBM Corp. IBM Plex is distributed under the SIL

## Design references

OpenDyslexic and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono were used for visual comparison and
legibility principles. Their outlines were not copied into Local.

LocalMono Nerd Font Mono adds glyphs from Nerd Fonts v3.4.0. Those added glyphs
retain their upstream copyrights and licenses. The package includes the pinned
Nerd Fonts notices and license files.
